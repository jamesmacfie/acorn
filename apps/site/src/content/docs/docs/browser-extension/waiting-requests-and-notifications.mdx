When an agent stops to ask you something, such as permission to run a command, the browser extension
can tell you while you're on another page. This page covers the toolbar badge, the **Needs you**
list, and notifications from your operating system.

## The toolbar badge

The Acorn button in the toolbar shows a number: how many requests are waiting in the projects of the
page you're on. A page's projects are the task it belongs to, and the projects its sites suggest
([Project sites](/docs/browser-extension/project-sites/)).

The badge works with the panel closed, and goes to zero at once when the Node stops answering. It needs
no access to the site, only the tab's address.

## The Needs you list

**Needs you** lists every request waiting on the Node, across all its projects, not only this
page's. It's at the top of Home while something waits, and a task's **Agents** tab starts with that
task's requests. On every other screen, the amber **Needs you** button in the top bar shows the count
and opens the same list.

<SidebarPicture id="sidebar-home" />

Each row names the request, then its conversation and task. Click a permission or a single choice to
see its detail and buttons, and answer it there: two clicks from any screen. The row shows what you
chose, such as **Allowed once**, then leaves. A form or any other request opens its conversation
instead, and **Open chat** does the same from an open row.

So the badge says what waits for this page, and the list says what waits anywhere.

## Notifications from your operating system

Notifications are off until you turn them on:

1. In the panel, open **Settings** > **Notifications**.
2. Turn on **When an agent needs you**.
3. Allow notifications when the browser asks.

You then get one notification for each request that waits in the projects of a page you have open in
front, in any window. It says only **An agent is asking for permission.** or **An agent has a question
for you.**, then **Open Acorn to answer.** It never shows the request, a project or task name, or the
page. Click it to open the panel on that conversation. If the browser won't open the panel from a
notification, the conversation is there the next time you open the panel.

Turning notifications off in the browser's own settings turns them off here too.

## Limits

- The badge and notifications cover requests in the projects of the page in front. The list covers the
  whole Node.
- A request from an archived task isn't counted, because it has no conversation to open.
- The extension notifies only for requests from the Node it's using.
- It reads at most the 100 oldest waiting requests on the Node.
