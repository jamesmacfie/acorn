The Account plugin signs you in to an account service and, when you turn on remote access, registers
your Node there. A client on another network can then reach the Node through a relay, without a VPN or
an open port.

## Sign in

Open **Settings** > **Machines** > **Account** and click **Sign in**. The page shows an address and a
code. Open the address in a browser, sign in with GitHub, and approve the code. The terminal client
prints the same address and code.

A build of acorn signs in only when `ACORN_ACCOUNT_URL` names an account service, which is a plain
`https` address, or `http` for a service on the same computer. Set it in the Node's environment.

You sign in on the Node whose settings are open. The sign-in belongs to that Node and its computer, and
it ends when you click **Sign out**.

## Turn on remote access

Turn on **Remote access** to register this Node with your account. The Node connects to a relay, and the
page says which one and since when. Turning it off ends the connection and every stream through it at
once, and removes the Node from your account. Signing out turns it off first and unpairs every phone,
because a phone reaches the Node only through your account. Computers stay paired.

Remote access doesn't let anyone in. A client still pairs with a code and uses its own device
credential, and the account service can't approve a pairing or read what crosses the relay. Phone
previews are the one exception, below.

If the Node's environment or `node.json` already names a relay, the switch is off and says why.

## Turn on phone previews

With remote access on, **Phone previews** lets a phone show a task's running app. It's off until you
turn it on, and turning remote access off turns it off too.

Each preview gets a public web address under a domain the account service controls. A browser trusts any
certificate a public authority issues for that address, so whoever runs the account service could get one,
read the page as it passes through the relay, and use the app as you. Turn previews on only if you trust
the account service with what your app shows and does.
