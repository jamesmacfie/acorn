The Account plugin signs you in to an account service and, when you turn on remote access, registers
your Node there. A client on another network can then reach the Node through a relay, without a VPN or
an open port.

  <Fragment slot="needs">A build of acorn that has an account service to talk to. A build with none says so on the page and offers no sign-in.</Fragment>

## Sign in

Open **Settings** > **Machines** > **Account** and click **Sign in**. The page shows an address and a
code. Open the address in a browser, sign in with GitHub, and approve the code. The terminal client
prints the same address and code.

You sign in on the Node whose settings are open. The sign-in belongs to that Node and its computer, and
it ends when you click **Sign out**.

## Turn on remote access

Turn on **Remote access** to register this Node with your account. The Node connects to a relay, and the
page says which one and since when. Turning it off ends the connection and every stream through it at
once, and removes the Node from your account. Signing out turns it off first.

Remote access doesn't let anyone in. A client still pairs with a code and uses its own device
credential, and the account service can't approve a pairing or read what crosses the relay.

If the Node's environment or `node.json` already names a relay, the switch is off and says why.
