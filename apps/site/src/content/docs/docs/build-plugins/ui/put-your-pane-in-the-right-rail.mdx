The right rail is the pane switcher: one button for each pane a task can show. There's no separate
contribution for it. A plugin reaches the right rail by adding a pane, and this page says how each
part of the pane's entry shows up there.

## The result

The host draws the switcher, so the picture is the window's own: see the right edge of the window in
[The window: top bar, rails, and drawer](/docs/using/the-window-top-bar-rails-and-drawer/). Your pane's
button sits among acorn's own, with your icon and your label in its tooltip.

## Before you start

You need a pane ([Add a pane](/docs/build-plugins/ui/add-a-pane/)).

## Steps

1. **Give the pane an icon and a label.** `glyph` is the button's icon, a Lucide name or a brand
   mark, and `label` is its name in the tooltip and the palette. `order` places the button: lower
   comes first.

   <ExampleCode file="acme-tracker/acorn-plugin.json" pick={['contributions.frames#acme-tracker']} />

2. **Give it a chord.** Add a command that opens the pane, and a keybinding for it
   ([Add commands and keybindings](/docs/build-plugins/ui/add-commands-and-keybindings/)), so the
   person can bring the pane up from the keyboard.

   <ExampleCode file="acme-tracker/acorn-plugin.json" pick={['contributions.commands#open', 'contributions.keybindings']} />

3. **Check it.** Save, open a task, and find the button on the right edge of the window. Click it to
   show the pane. Right-click it for **Pin**, which keeps the pane in the task's row, and the other
   layout actions.

## What the person controls

- **Pinning, showing, and moving.** The person pins the pane, shows or hides it, and moves it in the
  row. acorn saves that per task and Node, keyed by your pane's `id`. Renaming the `id` loses it.
- **Which tasks offer it.** `availability` names a route of yours that says, for each task, whether
  the pane applies. A pane without it is offered on every task.
- **The whole switcher.** A plugin can offer a replacement for the switcher itself, which the person
  chooses in Settings ([Replace the top bar, rail, or pane switcher](/docs/build-plugins/ui/replace-the-top-bar-rail-or-pane-switcher/)).

## Mistakes

- `glyph` is a name, not a path or an SVG. An unknown name draws as text.
- `showInSwitcher: false` hides the button. The pane still opens from a command.

## In the terminal

The terminal client has no right rail. Panes are a strip of labels above the one pane it shows, and
your pane is there by its `label`.

## Limits

- A plugin can't add a button that isn't a pane, or reorder acorn's own.
- Status marks on the button come from the `core:pane` point
  ([Mark tasks, sources, and panes](/docs/build-plugins/ui/mark-tasks-sources-and-panes/)).
