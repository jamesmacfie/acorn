Use `HistoryMark` at the start of a commit row to draw its place in the graph. `slot` says its lane and the lines that join it to the rows above and below.

## Mistakes

- Leaving the parents and refs out of the row's text. The terminal draws a dot and a lane number, so the text has to name them.
