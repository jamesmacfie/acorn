Use `Pane` as the root of a tree that fills a pane or a region. Its header, tabs, and footer keep their height, and the body takes the rows that are left.

## Related

- [`Stack`](/docs/components/grouping/stack/), for a run of content inside the pane, or `grow` for the part that takes the room.
- [`SectionHeader`](/docs/components/grouping/section-header/), for the pane's header line.
- [`Toolbar`](/docs/components/grouping/toolbar/), for actions along the top or the bottom.

## Mistakes

- Putting two `Stack grow` children in one `Pane`. Only one region can take the room that is left.
- Scrolling the whole pane. Let the body scroll, so the header and footer stay put, and set `TabPanel scroll={false}` when a child such as `Log` scrolls itself.
