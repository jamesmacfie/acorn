Use `Stack` for a column of content. `gap` takes a space role, never a number, and `grow` makes the stack the region rather than a run of content inside one.

## Mistakes

- Passing `gap={8}` or a CSS length. A gap is one of the space roles, and anything else is dropped.
- Setting `grow` on a stack that sits inside another run of content. `grow` means the stack fills its box, so it needs a `Pane` or a `Stack grow` around it.
