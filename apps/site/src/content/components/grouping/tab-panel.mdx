`TabPanel` holds the content for one tab of a `Tabs` strip. It draws when `active` matches its `id`, and scrolls its content unless you set `scroll={false}`.

## Mistakes

- Leaving `scroll` on when the child scrolls itself, such as a `Log`. Two scrollers in one region fight over the wheel and the keys.
