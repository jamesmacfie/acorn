Use `Inline` for a run of short things on one line: a label and its badge, a row of buttons. It wraps when it runs out of room.

## Mistakes

- Leaving out `gap` between two runs of text. In the terminal two runs with nothing between them read as one word, so give it `gap="inline"`.
- Relying on `even` in the terminal. The terminal ignores it, because equal columns mean nothing in a row of cells.
