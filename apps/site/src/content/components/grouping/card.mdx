Use `Card` to frame one item, such as a turn in a review or a result. A `stripe` tones its leading edge, and `onPress` makes the whole card one control.

## Mistakes

- Giving a card `onPress` and also a button inside it. The card is one tab stop, so put the action on the card or on a button, not both.
- Toning a card with a color. `stripe` takes `accent`, `ok`, `warn`, or `danger`.
