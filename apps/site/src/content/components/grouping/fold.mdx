Use `Fold` for content a reader opens when they need it, such as a list of commits. The desktop builds the body the first time it opens and keeps it, so a closed fold costs nothing.

## Mistakes

- Holding the open state in your tree when the reader is the only one who changes it. Leave `open` out and set `defaultOpen`, and the fold keeps its own state. Pass `open` with `onOpenChange` only when your code also opens or closes it.
