Use `SettingsSection` for one group of settings, with a label, a description, and a `SettingRow` per setting. Its `id` is an anchor on the page, so search can land on it.

## Mistakes

- Reusing an `id` on one page. Two sections with one anchor send search to the first.
- Drawing a danger zone with a red frame. Set `tone="danger"`, and the terminal draws the label in the danger color instead.
