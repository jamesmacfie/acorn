Use `Modal` for a short task the reader finishes or cancels, such as confirming a delete. Put its content in `ModalBody` and its buttons in `ModalActions`. Escape and the close button send `onDismiss`.

## Mistakes

- Leaving out `onDismiss`. It's required, because a reader must always be able to leave.
