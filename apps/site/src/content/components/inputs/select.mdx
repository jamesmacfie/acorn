Use `Select` for one choice from a short list. Each option is `{ value, label }`, and `onChange` sends the chosen value.

## Related

- [`SegmentedControl`](/docs/components/inputs/segmented-control/), for two to four choices that stay in view.
- [`Picker`](/docs/components/inputs/picker/), for a long list the reader searches.

## Mistakes

- Using a select for a long list of people or files. Use a `Picker`, which filters as the reader types.
