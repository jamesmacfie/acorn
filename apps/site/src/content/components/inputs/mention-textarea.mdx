Use `MentionTextarea` for a comment that can name people. Pass the names in `mentions`, and typing `@` opens a menu of them.

## Mistakes

- Reading the text from a tree. Its `onInput` isn't one of the twelve events, and its `onSubmit` sends nothing, so a tree can't read what was typed. Use a `Composer`, whose `onSubmit` sends the text.
- Expecting the terminal to highlight a completed mention. It draws the text plain, with the menu under the field.
