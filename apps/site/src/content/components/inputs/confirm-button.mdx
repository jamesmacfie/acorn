Use `ConfirmButton` for an action that's hard to undo, such as delete. The first press arms it and shows `confirmLabel`, and the second sends `onConfirm`.

## Related

## Mistakes

- Listening for `onPress` to act. Act on `onConfirm`, which comes only after the second press.
