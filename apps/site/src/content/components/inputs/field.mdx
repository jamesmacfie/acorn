Wrap a control in `Field` to label it. The label names the control, and the hint, the error, and `help` are read with it. `required` marks the label with an asterisk; give the control `required` too, so a screen reader says it.

## Mistakes

- Putting two controls in one field. The label names one, and with two it names none.
