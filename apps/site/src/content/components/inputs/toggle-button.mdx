Use `ToggleButton` for an on and off state that reads as a button, such as wrapping lines. `pressed` sets it, and `onPress` reports a press, so your tree flips `pressed` in its handler.

## Mistakes

- Waiting for `onPressedChange` in a tree. It isn't one of the twelve events, so it's dropped. Flip `pressed` on `onPress`.
