Use `Picker` for a choice from a long list. Pass `items`, and the picker filters them as the reader types and sends `onPick` with the chosen id. Give rows a `group`, with each group's rows together, and a label shows over each group the filter keeps.

## Mistakes

- Passing `results` to filter. It's a function, so a tree passes `items` and the picker filters them itself.
