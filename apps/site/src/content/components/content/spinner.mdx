Use `Spinner` while something loads or runs. Give it a `label`, which names the work for a screen reader. The terminal draws a braille cycle, or `…` where motion is off.

## Mistakes

- Leaving a spinner as the only sign of a failure. Replace it with an `Alert` when the work fails.
