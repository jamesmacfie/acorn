Use `Meter` for a fraction from 0 to 1, such as context used or budget spent. `mark` puts a marker at a threshold, and `shape="pie"` draws one small pie.

## Related

## Mistakes

- Passing a percentage. `value` is a fraction, so 62 percent is `0.62`.
- Leaving out `label`. The meter needs one for a screen reader.
