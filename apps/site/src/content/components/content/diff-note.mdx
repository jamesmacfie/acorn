Use `DiffNote` as a child of a `StackedDiff` to pin a note under one of its lines. `side` and `line` name the line, `tone` and `label` say what kind of note it is, and `author` and `title` fill its header. Its children are the body and your own buttons. A reader can fold the note to its header. To fold it from your own button, such as **Got it**, pass `open` and `onOpenChange`, and the note's fold is yours.

## Related

- [`StackedDiff`](/docs/components/content/stacked-diff/), the diff a note sits in. Its `onActivate` hears the ✦ button on any line.

## Mistakes

- Putting a `DiffNote` anywhere but directly in a `StackedDiff`. Outside one it isn't drawn.
- Naming a line the patch doesn't have. The note draws after the last line, with its line number in the header, so check the line first.
