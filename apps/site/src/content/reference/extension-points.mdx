An extension point is a place in one plugin's view that another plugin may fill. The owner opens it,
and you fill it with an `extensions` entry naming the point as `<owner>:<point>`. Nothing reaches into a
view that didn't open a point, and nothing runs your code inside another plugin: you bring a tree from
your own client bundle, or rows and marks from your own routes, and acorn draws them where the owner
said.

Each point takes one of two things:

- **A tree.** Name a `remote` entry your client bundle passes to `mountTree`. A `replace` point draws one
  contributor: the person picks when two match, and until then the owner's own view draws. A `stack`
  point draws several, up to its cap. `matches` narrows your tree to the keys it can draw, exactly or as
  `prefix*` or `*suffix`.
- **Marks.** Name an `items` route on your Node half. acorn POSTs `{ keys }` for what it's drawing, and
  your route answers `{ items: [{ key, severity, text, icon? }] }`, with `severity` one of `info`,
  `warn`, and `danger`. Text is cut at 200 characters.

If the owner isn't installed, or is turned off, your contribution draws nothing and reports no error.

## An example for each point

### Points `core` opens

#### `core:link-preview`

A preview of an HTTPS link, drawn where acorn shows the link.

```json manifest
{
  "client": "./client.js",
  "contributions": {
    "extensions": [
      {
        "id": "core-link-preview",
        "point": "core:link-preview",
        "label": "Acme link preview",
        "remote": "linkPreview",
        "matches": [
          "https://acme.example/*"
        ]
      }
    ]
  }
}
```

## Mistakes

- A `point` that isn't `<owner>:<point>` is refused when the manifest loads.
- Name exactly one of `items`, `remote`, `frame`, and `route`. Which one fits depends on the point.
- A tree reads its own data through your own routes, and only gets the props listed above. It can't read
  the owner's data.
