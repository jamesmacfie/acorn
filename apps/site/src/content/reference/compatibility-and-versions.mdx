acorn has one version number a plugin cares about: the plugin API major. Your manifest's `apiVersion`
says which majors your plugin works with, and acorn loads it only when that range includes its own.

{/* facts */}

## Writing apiVersion

`apiVersion` is a range over majors, not a version of your plugin:

- One major, such as `"@API_MAJOR@"`.
- A list of majors, such as `"2 || 3"`.
- An inclusive span, such as `"2-4"`.

Declare a major only after you've tested your plugin against an acorn that speaks it. A range that
doesn't parse is refused, never read as "any".

## The promise

A plugin that loads under a plugin API major keeps loading under it. The promise covers the manifest
contract, the published declarations in `acorn-plugin-types`, and the published SDK, `acorn-plugin-sdk`.
A change that removes something from them, or changes its shape, needs a new major.

Adding is not breaking. A new contribution key, a new field, or a new verb arrives within a major, and an
older acorn skips a key it doesn't know rather than refusing your manifest.

## What isn't promised

- That the major won't move.
- That an older major keeps working after it moves.
- A deprecation period before it does.
- That an older acorn draws a new field or menu location inside a key it knows. A release that predates
  a value may refuse that one entry.

The npm versions of `acorn-plugin-types` and `acorn-plugin-sdk` are separate from the major. Your
plugin's own `version` is yours.
