`contributions` lists what your plugin adds to acorn, by kind. Each kind is a key holding a list, and
each list has a cap. Past the cap, a contribution stops being an integration and becomes an app inside
someone else's window, so acorn refuses the manifest.

Leave out any kind you don't use. A key this acorn doesn't know is reported and skipped, so a manifest
written for a newer acorn still loads here with less in it.

{/* facts */}

Most kinds are data acorn draws or runs: a badge, a palette entry, a menu row, a schedule. None of them
runs your code inside acorn's window. When one needs live data, it names a route on your Node half, and
acorn calls it. Your own UI, a pane or a panel body, is a `frames` entry drawn by your client bundle.
