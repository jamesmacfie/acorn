## Shortcuts this programme refuses

Do not truncate canonical terminal, transcript, document, workflow, or result content to manufacture
a plateau. Do not cap or erase dirty drafts and undo state. Do not clear offline caches or shorten
established content budgets without an explicit product decision. Do not replace freshness or body
identity with a convenient TTL. One departing shared consumer cannot cancel surviving consumers.
No private core imports, relaxed capability boundaries, ambient cross-Node operations, speculative
future architecture, or changed applied migrations. No paid provider work, private-content logging,
normal-profile inspection, or external messages are required for these probes.

Count reductions are distinct from CPU, retained heap, resident memory, and visible latency.
Simulated workers, jsdom, hidden windows, historical Sentry traces without release identity, and
short fixture runs cannot prove a native speedup or several days of stable use. Preserve measured
regressions and failure artifacts beside improvements.

## Verify before building

- Check whether merged main or preceding units already solve the residual.
- Reproduce the cost at the current production owner with exact supported behavior.
- Define the missing policy or compatibility decision and review it before implementation.
- Add a separate sequential assignment only after evidence supports selecting it.
